\# Design Tokens



Design tokens provide shared values for visual consistency across products and interfaces.



\## Color



Define semantic roles rather than using raw colors directly.



Examples:



\- `color.background`

\- `color.surface`

\- `color.text.primary`

\- `color.text.secondary`

\- `color.border`

\- `color.action.primary`

\- `color.action.danger`

\- `color.status.success`

\- `color.status.warning`

\- `color.status.error`

\- `color.status.info`



\## Typography



Define a consistent type scale.



Recommended token groups:



\- `font.family.sans`

\- `font.family.mono`

\- `font.size.xs`

\- `font.size.sm`

\- `font.size.md`

\- `font.size.lg`

\- `font.size.xl`

\- `font.size.2xl`

\- `font.weight.regular`

\- `font.weight.medium`

\- `font.weight.semibold`

\- `font.weight.bold`

\- `line.height.tight`

\- `line.height.normal`

\- `line.height.relaxed`



\## Spacing



Use a predictable spacing scale.



Examples:



\- `space.1`

\- `space.2`

\- `space.3`

\- `space.4`

\- `space.6`

\- `space.8`

\- `space.12`

\- `space.16`



\## Radius



Examples:



\- `radius.none`

\- `radius.sm`

\- `radius.md`

\- `radius.lg`

\- `radius.xl`

\- `radius.full`



\## Elevation



Examples:



\- `shadow.none`

\- `shadow.sm`

\- `shadow.md`

\- `shadow.lg`

\- `shadow.xl`



\## Motion



Define motion consistently.



Examples:



\- `motion.duration.fast`

\- `motion.duration.normal`

\- `motion.duration.slow`

\- `motion.easing.standard`

\- `motion.easing.emphasized`



\## Accessibility



Tokens must support accessible contrast, visible focus states, readable typography, and reduced-motion preferences.



\## Usage Rule



Prefer semantic tokens over raw values when implementing reusable components.
